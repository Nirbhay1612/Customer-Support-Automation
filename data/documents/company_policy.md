# Company Policy

## Overview

This document contains general information about the company's customer support policies.

## Customer Support

Customers can contact support through the available customer support channels.

Support requests should include relevant information such as the order ID when the request is related to an order.

## Account Information

Customers should keep their account information accurate and up to date.

Sensitive account information should not be shared publicly.

## Order Information

Customers should provide their order ID when requesting information about a specific order.

## Important

If the available information does not answer a customer's question, the AI assistant should not invent an answer.

The request should be escalated to human support when necessary.
