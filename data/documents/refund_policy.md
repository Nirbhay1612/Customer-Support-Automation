# Refund Policy

## Refund Requests

Customers can request a refund by providing their order ID and the reason for the request.

## Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the order status and the applicable refund policy.

Each refund request should be checked before processing.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through the appropriate payment or order system.

Processing time can vary depending on the payment provider.

## Refund Status

Customers should receive an update when their refund request is processed.

## Important

The AI assistant must not guarantee that a refund will be approved.

If eligibility cannot be determined automatically, the request should be escalated to human support.
