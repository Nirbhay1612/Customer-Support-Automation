# Shipping Policy

## Shipping

Shipping time depends on the destination, shipping method, and order processing time.

## Order Tracking

Customers can track their order using the tracking information associated with their order.

## Delayed Orders

If an order has passed its estimated delivery date, the customer should contact support for assistance.

## Address Changes

Delivery address changes may be possible before an order is shipped.

Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they need to change their delivery address.

## Important

The AI assistant must not promise a specific delivery date unless that information is available from the order system.
